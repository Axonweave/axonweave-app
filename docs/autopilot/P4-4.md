# P4-4 - Study guides, glossaries, formula sheets, question banks (Phase 4 M4, backend only)

## What was built
- Four generators on the M0 engine and registry, each with validated settings (unknown fields are 422), a versioned prompt (v1) and source attribution: `study_guide`, `glossary`, `formula_sheet`, `question_bank`. They are created through the existing `POST /study/generations` (`generation_type`).
- **Study guide**: heading hierarchy check (level 1 to 3, no skipped level, no empty section or duplicate heading), objectives (an objective with no valid source is kept only as `generated_recommendation: true`), prerequisites, takeaways, practice questions (no answers). Edits re-run the hierarchy rules.
- **Glossary**: repeated terms are merged (first definition wins, references and related terms unioned), sorted alphabetically, unsupported terms dropped. `GET /study/materials/{id}/glossary?q=&topic=&order=` for search, topic filter and sort.
- **Formula sheet** (`app/study/latex.py`): unsafe LaTeX (links, macros, file/IO commands, HTML) is dropped; syntax problems and undefined variables are kept and flagged `needs_review` with fixed note codes; flags are recomputed by the backend, also on edit. The sheet is always `verification: unverified`.
- **Question bank**: reuses M3 `question_rules` and storage. Answer keys, explanations and rubrics exist only in `quiz_questions`; the material content is the public view (no keys) with a topic > difficulty outline. `GET /study/materials/{id}/questions?q=&topic=&difficulty=&question_type=` filters it. No migration.
- Docs: D-97 to D-99, AUTOPILOT_LOG lines, TASKS_Phase4 M4 ticked.

## Decisions made (with reasons)
- No per-type routes: the generic endpoint is how M1 to M3 shipped (D-97).
- Invalid formulas: drop unsafe, flag the rest, never claim correctness, per spec 10.4 (D-98).
- Bank storage on the quiz tables: keeps key protection and avoids a migration (D-99). Bank question editing and quiz-from-bank are M5/M6 (not in M4).
- `test_echo_is_not_available_in_production` lists the production generators, so it was extended with the four new keys (the expected list is exact by design).
